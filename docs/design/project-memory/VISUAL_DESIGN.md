## Durable Context

- Portfolio project pages share the Yudi AI Lab shell, typography hierarchy,
  restrained framing, and responsive behavior.
- Project-specific identity may appear through bounded typographic marks, subtle
  evidence accents, and first-party product imagery without introducing a
  parallel palette or component system.
- Workflow evidence should remain inspectable and uncropped, with intrinsic media
  geometry reserved before lazy loading.
- Long public process documents may use quiet evidence-sheet treatments and
  in-page reading dialogs that remain subordinate to the portfolio narrative.

## Constraints

- Preserve the shared visual shell when adding future portfolio projects.
- Use first-party product evidence for product-specific storytelling.
- Keep decorative detail restrained and prevent it from competing with titles,
  navigation, or evidence content.
